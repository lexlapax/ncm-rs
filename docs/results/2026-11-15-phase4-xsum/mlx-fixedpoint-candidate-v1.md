# XSum-only Metal fixed-point sampler: initial candidate

At commit `46455f3`, all three real-Metal XSum categorical tests passed, covering controlled FP64 reference draws, exact half-boundary words, zero and maximum words, tiny leading weights, nonfinite values and invalid shapes. TinyStories remains on its existing sampler. Linux full `tools/check.sh --cuda` passed before Metal, with 18 operator cases, as did strict-cuDNN Clippy. On 32 CUDA passages the XSum-specific hook reproduced the prior strict-CUDA seeded result byte-for-byte.

The repeated 32-passage Metal-versus-CUDA seeded comparison still failed exact agreement, but improved from 284 to 102 differing codes out of 6,400 and from 106 to 51 differing final IDs out of 1,536. The [candidate CLI result](mlx-fixedpoint-candidate-cli-v1.json) has SHA-256 `b427a99483405773eb5cf1d51d5b3cb16b512a4664612aa0a825633c28fafee6`; output SHA-256 is `0b2fc6e843bdd1906b3a702b3480ec448e7286d95eaf5871dc8d244c1382714b`. Three rows diverged: their first flattened code positions were `(0,58)`, `(25,144)` and `(29,22)`, with 35, 8 and 59 changed codes respectively. Later differences can cascade.

In the [candidate shared-history diagnostic](mlx-fixedpoint-candidate-shared-v1.json), SHA-256 `a02084b68e351daa3810d7b831ed258eaf68bb1ebce6ecc2432678b8d7d89d6b`, all 26,214,400 head logits remained within the existing band. Same-CUDA-logit decisions differed once in 6,400; forced-Metal-logit decisions differed three times. At row 25 position 144, the fixed-point Metal result agrees with the independently calculated FP64 inverse-CDF choice while CUDA differs. The other two free-generation first differences (rows 0 and 29) appear on forced Metal logits but not when sampling the saved CUDA logits. The candidate also introduces the row-29 divergence, which the earlier Metal sampler did not exhibit; do not interpret the smaller aggregate count as universal improvement.

Raw test/build logs and outputs are retained on Mac under `reports/phase4-xsum-fixedpoint-candidate-{mlx,cli}-v1/`, and copied locally under `reports/phase4-xsum-fixedpoint-candidate-captured-v1/`. The fixed-point sampler retains FP32 exponentials and truncates weights below `2^-48`; the original failure remains visible. Full real-Metal regression, stochastic task quality and repeated latency/memory comparison remain pending. This limited diagnostic does not qualify the candidate or establish universal numerical equivalence.
